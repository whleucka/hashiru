# v1.14.2

The system menu gets a Devices tab: hashiru-tui v0.4.0. And do not disturb.

## The system menu

- New **Devices** tab in Hardware, one list of what's plugged in:
  - **Drives**: removable disks and their partitions, never the system
    disk. `m` mounts, `u` unmounts and `o` opens a partition in Thunar;
    `e` ejects (unmount everything, lock what's unlocked, power off). All
    through udisks, no sudo. An encrypted stick asks its passphrase in the
    terminal, then mounts.
  - **USB**: devices as a tree under their hubs, with their speeds; `R`
    resets one through `usb --reset`.
  - **Thunderbolt**: bolt's devices and security level; `a` authorizes,
    `s` stores, `f` forgets. A device plugged in but not authorized marks
    the tab.
  - It reloads every five seconds while it's showing, so a stick appears
    when you plug it in.
- **Power** shows a USB-C charger's negotiated wattage and what the laptop
  draws: `charger 92W USB-C PD  drawing 65W`.

## Desktop

- Do not disturb on `SUPER+SHIFT+.`: mako hides all but critical
  notifications until it's turned off, then shows what it held back.

## Upgrading

`hashiru update`. Stage 99 rebuilds hashiru-tui at the new pin, and stage
45 brings the mako mode and the bind. The ISO installs v0.4.0 from the
start.
