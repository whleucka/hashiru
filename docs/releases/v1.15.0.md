# v1.15.0

Window, group and scratchpad binds move onto hjkl chords, and a laptop
on a dying battery powers off instead of suspending.

## Keybinds

- Resize is SUPER+CTRL+H/J/K/L (hold to repeat). SUPER+-/= and the
  SUPER+R resize submap are gone.
- Pulling a neighbour into a group moves from SUPER+CTRL+hjkl to
  SUPER+ALT+hjkl.
- Group tabs: CTRL+SHIFT+H/L for previous/next (was SUPER+P/N),
  CTRL+SHIFT+ALT+H/L moves the active tab left/right (new).
- Scratchpad: SUPER+= sends a window into it, SUPER+- drops it back
  (were SUPER+Insert/Delete). SUPER+Home is gone; CTRL+` toggles it.
- herdr gives CTRL+SHIFT+H/L up to Hyprland: tabs are ALT+[ / ALT+],
  reordered with ALT+{ / ALT+}, jumped to with ALT+1..9. Workspaces jump
  with CTRL+1..9 (was ALT+1..9).

## Power

- `dock-power-watch` runs at login. When a Thunderbolt dock is connected
  but the laptop is on battery — typical after waking from suspend with
  the dock plugged in — it raises a critical notification to replug it.
- UPower's critical action is PowerOff. Swap is zram-only, so the machine
  cannot hibernate and the default fell back to suspend, which kept
  draining until the battery died uncleanly.

## Upgrading

`hashiru update`. Stage 10 installs `/etc/UPower/UPower.conf.d/99-hashiru.conf`
and restarts upower; stage 45 restows the binds. `dock-power-watch` starts
on the next login.
