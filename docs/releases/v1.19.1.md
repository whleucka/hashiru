# v1.19.1

kitty can flip a split, and the weather stops saying +-0°.

## Binds

- CTRL+SHIFT+O switches the focused kitty pane's split between horizontal and
  vertical (`layout_action rotate`). It takes the chord from kitty's stock
  "pass selection to program".

## Fixes

- Waybar's weather shows 0°C where wttr.in reports +-0°C or -0°C. The fix is
  applied when the text is drawn, so a forecast cached before the update shows
  correctly too.

## Upgrading

`hashiru update`, then restart kitty.
