# v1.10.1

Patch release. kitty stops taking orders from whatever it prints, and the update
progress bar says what it is waiting on.

## Security

- kitty remote control is `socket-only`. `yes` also obeyed escape codes written
  to the tty, so anything that printed could `launch` or `send-text` locally.
  `ssh.conf` and its `forward_remote_control` are gone for the same reason.

## Update

- The progress bar labels the two long steps, `pacman -Syu` and
  `grub-mkconfig`, instead of sitting at 0% with no label and reading as a hang.

## Desktop

- kitty: font size 15.
- `super+shift+t` (btop) and `super+shift+u` (update) open at font size 11.

## Upgrading

`hashiru update`, then restart kitty: open windows keep the old setting. A
`~/.config/hashiru/kitty/*.conf` that sets `allow_remote_control` still wins.
