# v1.14.1

The system menu gets a Hardware area: hashiru-tui v0.3.0.

## The system menu

- New Hardware area, with three tabs:
  - **Monitors**: every output's mode, position, scale, rotation and
    on/off. Change them, then apply: it writes
    `~/.config/hashiru/hypr/monitors.lua` and reloads Hyprland, and you
    have 15 seconds to press `y` or the old layout comes back. Rules for
    displays that aren't plugged in are kept; a `monitors.lua` you wrote by
    hand is asked about and copied to `monitors.lua.bak` first.
  - **Power**: battery charge, health and cycles, TLP's profile (`p`
    switches it), idle timeouts for AC and battery (`i` changes them, `I`
    goes back to Hashiru's), and charge thresholds through `hashiru charge`
    (`c`, `C` for off).
  - **Firmware**: fwupd's devices, pending updates first; `u` runs
    `fwupdmgr update`, `F` fetches new metadata.
- Deep links reach a tab: `hashiru-tui hardware.power`, `logs.journal`.
- The sidebar shows area names; `1`-`9` and `0` still jump to the first ten.

## Upgrading

`hashiru update`. Stage 99 rebuilds hashiru-tui at the new pin. The ISO now
installs v0.3.0 from the start.
